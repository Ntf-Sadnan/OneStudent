28. EXPANDED NATIONAL PLATFORM SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------

Position the product clearly as:
“Bangladesh’s National Digital Learning Platform”

The platform should connect formal education, printed books, digital lessons,
assessments, lifelong academic identity, guardian oversight, practical skills,
and the wider EdTech ecosystem.

Core additions:

1. Video-based lessons
- Every chapter can include high-quality video lessons.
- Videos should support both classroom learning and self-study.
- Students can continue directly from the relevant chapter or topic.

2. QR-connected books
- Printed textbooks and learning materials contain QR codes.
- Scanning a lesson QR opens the exact related video or digital class.
- Scanning a test QR opens the related quiz, exam, or model test directly.
- Students can move from a physical book to a lesson or exam with one scan.

3. Lifelong national student ID
- Every student receives a unique digital student ID.
- The ID remains with the learner throughout their full education career.
- School changes, class changes, exams, certificates, and achievements remain
  connected to the same trusted identity.

4. Unified academic profile
- Every student has a personal academic profile.
- Exams, marks, attendance, progress, certificates, strengths, weak topics,
  skills, and achievements are stored together.
- The profile becomes a lifelong academic record and learning passport.

5. Guardian device control and monitoring
- Guardians can monitor learning progress, attendance, exams, and study time.
- Guardians can manage learning schedules and appropriate app/device access.
- Controls must be transparent, age-appropriate, privacy-conscious, and focused
  on healthy digital learning rather than surveillance.

6. Skill-based learning
- Students can learn beyond the academic curriculum.
- Include practical pathways such as farming, English speaking, digital skills,
  entrepreneurship, vocational foundations, and life skills.
- Skill pathways can include videos, projects, assessments, and certificates.

7. EdTech collaboration ecosystem
- The platform can bring trusted EdTech companies and education providers
  together under shared national standards.
- Partners can contribute approved content, tools, courses, assessments, and
  specialized learning experiences.
- Students should access this ecosystem through one trusted identity and profile.

8. National digital learning infrastructure
- Present the platform as shared digital infrastructure for students, schools,
  teachers, guardians, government, and education partners.
- Emphasize interoperability, accessibility, student safety, nationwide reach,
  trusted records, and continuity across the full education journey.

Required new visual sections:
- A printed textbook with visible QR codes linked to “Watch lesson” and
  “Start exam” digital cards.
- A lifelong student ID card connected to one unified academic profile.
- A guardian dashboard showing progress plus study-time/device controls.
- A skill-learning showcase with farming, English speaking, and digital skills.
- An EdTech partner ecosystem section showing multiple providers connecting
  through one national platform.
